# 5-minute user test: the Webster remodeler

Run this with one local business owner who has never seen the site. You watch
and take notes; you don't help, explain or defend. If they ask you something,
say "What would you do if I weren't here?"

## Before you start (1 minute)

- Use their phone if they'll let you; otherwise hand them yours. Most visitors
  will be on a phone.
- Open a private browser tab on the live home page:
  `https://www.stringhamwebdesign.com/`
- Have this sheet and a pen ready, plus a timer.

## Read this to them, word for word

> "You own a remodeling company in Webster. Your Google listing shows the
> wrong hours, and you've been missing calls. Someone gave you this website.
> Decide whether you'd trust this person and ask for help. Think out loud as
> you go: say what you're looking at, what you're wondering, and what you'd
> do. There are no wrong answers; I'm testing the website, not you."

Start the timer. Don't say anything else until it's over, or until 5 minutes
are up.

## What to watch for

Write down the time and what they said or did at each moment.

| Moment | What to note |
|---|---|
| First 5 seconds | Can they say what this is, who it's for and where? Ask afterwards if you missed it. |
| The first scroll | Do they scroll, or tap the green button straight away? |
| Hesitations | Any pause longer than about 3 seconds, a frown, or "hmm". Note where on the page it happened. |
| Back and forth | Scrolling up again usually means something didn't answer a question. Note what they went back to. |
| The work | Do they look at Common Ground? Do they tap "Visit the live site"? |
| The price | Do they find the $300 Listing Fix? Do they read "you stay the owner, never your password"? |
| Trust | Anything they say about the photo, the phone number, League City, or "is this a real person?" |
| The form | Do they start it? Which field do they stop at? Do they say what they expect to happen after sending? |
| Giving up | If they'd leave, where and why, in their words. |

## After 5 minutes, ask (about 2 minutes)

1. "In one sentence, what does this person do?"
2. "Would you send this person your details? Why, or why not?"
3. "What almost stopped you?" (or "What did stop you?")
4. "What would you expect to happen after you send the form?"
5. "Was anything confusing, or did anything feel like a sales pitch?"

## Afterwards

- Write the three biggest hesitations at the top of the sheet, each with where
  on the page it happened.
- If they mention the phone number's area code, wanting reviews, wanting to see
  more work, or how long things take, mark it: those are the open items in the
  evidence list (local number, testimonials, more live work, timeline range).
- One owner is a signal, not a verdict. Run it with three before changing the
  page, and change what at least two of them stumbled on.
