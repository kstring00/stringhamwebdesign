## 3. Website Care (from $25/month)

Website Care is optional. Your website runs without it, in accounts that are yours. Website Care pays for our time running your site. It does not cover your domain, your hosting or any tool your site uses: you pay those bills directly to the companies that provide them.

**Three levels.** You choose one. Each level includes everything in the level below it.

- **Basic, $25 a month:** our time keeping your website fast, secure and online, working in your hosting account; a notice before your domain is due to renew (the registrar bills you); and up to 15 minutes of content edits a month.
- **Standard, $50 a month:** everything in Basic, with up to 45 minutes of content edits a month in place of 15; keeping your Google listing and directories accurate; and flagging new Google reviews to you within 3 business days.
- **Full, $85 a month:** everything in Standard, with up to 90 minutes of content edits a month in place of 45; and a one-page report each month of calls and requests.

**At every level**
- Content edits are changes to what is already on your site: fees, hours, photos, text. New pages and new sections are quoted separately before we do the work, as are redesigns, photography and paid ads.
- Unused edit time does not carry over to the next month.
- We never reply to reviews on a practice's behalf.
- You can change level or cancel anytime by email. The change takes effect from your next billing month. Payments for months already started aren't refunded.

**If you leave:** nothing has to be handed back, because your domain, hosting and code are already in your name. Remove us as a helper whenever you like. If we hold anything of yours (files, or an account opened for you before these terms), we hand it over within 14 days of your last paid month.
