# Privacy policy

This page explains what I collect when you use this site, why I collect it, who else touches it, and how to get rid of it. It is written the way I'd explain it to you on a call, because a policy you can't read protects nobody.

If you have a question about anything on this page, or you want to know what I hold about you, email me at [kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com](mailto:kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com). It comes to me, not a support queue.

## What I collect, and why

**When you send a project brief.** The quote form asks for your name, your email, your business name and what it does, what you have online now, what you want the site to accomplish, your rough timeline, how you prefer to be contacted, and which package you started from. I use it to reply to you and to scope the work. That's the whole purpose. It goes to my inbox and a copy goes to yours.

**When you're a client with a portal login.** The client portal holds your email address and sign-in records, your project details, the files either of us uploads, the messages we send each other, timesheets for the hours worked, and invoice records. I use it to deliver your project and to show you where the work stands. Each client can only see their own project — that separation is enforced by the database, not by the page you happen to be looking at.

**When you just visit.** Pages you view, links you click, how far you scroll, roughly where in the world you are, and what browser and device you're on. The next section covers this in full, because it's the part people most want to know about.

I don't ask for anything I don't need. There's no account to create just to read the site, and no newsletter signup collecting addresses for later.

## Analytics and session replay

This site uses **Microsoft Clarity** to understand how people actually use it. Clarity records page views, clicks, scrolling, and session replays — a reconstruction of how a visit moved through the site, so I can see where a page confuses people and fix it.

Three things are worth being clear about.

**Form fields are masked.** Clarity's default masking is on, and nothing on this site turns it off. What you type into the quote form is not captured in a replay. I see that a field was filled in, not what you put in it.

**The client portal is not recorded at all.** Anything under `/portal`, the sign-in page included, is excluded in code. No client dashboard, file list, message thread, timesheet or invoice is ever sent to Clarity.

**It uses cookies.** Clarity sets cookies to recognise a returning visitor across pages and sessions.

Microsoft is the one processing this, and what they do with it is governed by their own terms. Their [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) is the authoritative document, and it's worth a look if this matters to you.

### Opting out

Any of these works:

- **Block cookies for this site** in your browser settings. Clarity can't string a session together without them.
- **Use a private or incognito window.** The cookies die with the window.
- **Email me** at [kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com](mailto:kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com) and say you don't want to be recorded. I'll tell you exactly what I did about it.

None of these change what the site does for you. Nothing here is gated on being tracked.

## Who else handles your data

I'm one person, so the machinery belongs to other companies. These are the ones that touch your data, and what each one does:

- **Vercel** — hosts the site. Every page you load is served by them, so their servers see your requests and IP address.
- **Supabase** — the database, sign-in system and file storage behind the client portal. If you're a client, this is where your project data lives.
- **Resend** — delivers email. Your quote brief and its confirmation pass through them on the way to my inbox and yours, as do portal notifications.
- **Microsoft Clarity** — analytics and session replay, as described above.
- **Stripe** — payment processing. Card details go to Stripe directly and never touch my servers or my database. I only ever see whether an invoice was paid.

Each of these holds data because it needs it to do its job, and none of them get it to sell.

One note on Stripe, in the interest of not overstating things: the payment code is in place but is still running in test mode, so no real card has been charged through this site yet. It's listed here because it's wired up and will be live for client invoices.

## How long I keep things

**Quote briefs.** If we end up working together, the brief becomes part of the project record and follows the rule below. If we don't, I delete it two years after you sent it. Ask sooner and I'll delete it sooner.

**Client portal data.** It stays for the life of the project and for ninety days after handoff, so you can still pull a file or re-read a decision after we've finished. After that I archive or delete it. If you'd rather it went the day we're done, say so and it will.

**Analytics.** Microsoft holds this on their own retention schedule, which I don't control. Their privacy statement, linked above, is where that's set out.

**Email.** Correspondence sits in my inbox the way any business email does. I clear out old threads periodically rather than on a fixed schedule.

## Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email [kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com](mailto:kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com) and I'll answer within a few business days.

You don't need a reason and you don't need to use particular words. "What do you have on me?" is a complete request.

The one limit: if I'm legally required to keep something, like a record tied to an invoice, I'll keep that and tell you plainly which part I kept and why.

## What I don't do

I don't sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

I don't run ads on this site, and I don't hand your information to anyone who does.

I don't use what you send me to train anything, and I don't pass a client's project details to another client.
