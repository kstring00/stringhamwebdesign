# Privacy policy

This page explains what I collect when you use this site, why, who else touches it, and how to get rid of it. It's written the way I'd explain it on a call, because a policy you can't read protects nobody.

---

## Who I am

I'm Kyle Stringham. I run Stringham Web Design LLC out of League City, Texas. Questions about anything here, or about what I hold on you: email [kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com](mailto:kyle@stringhamwebdesign.com). It comes to me, not a support queue.

---

## What I collect, and why

**When you use the contact form.** Your name, your email, your business name, what you need, and your message. I use it to reply to you and to scope the work. That's the whole purpose. It goes to my inbox, and nowhere else.

**When you just visit.** Pages you view, links you click, how far you scroll, roughly where in the world you are, and what browser and device you're on. The next section covers this in full.

I don't ask for anything I don't need. There's no account to create, and no newsletter collecting addresses for later.

---

## Analytics and session replay

This site uses **Microsoft Clarity** to understand how people actually use it. Clarity records page views, clicks, scrolling, and session replays, a reconstruction of how a visit moved through the site, so I can see where a page confuses people and fix it.

**Form fields are masked.** Clarity's default masking is on, and nothing on this site turns it off. What you type into the contact form is not captured in a replay.

**It uses cookies** to recognise a returning visitor across pages and sessions.

Microsoft is the one processing this, under their own terms. Their [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) is the authoritative document.

### Opting out

- **Block cookies for this site** in your browser settings. Clarity can't string a session together without them.
- **Use a private or incognito window.** The cookies die with the window.
- **Email me** and say you don't want to be recorded. I'll tell you exactly what I did about it.

None of these change what the site does for you.

---

## Who else handles your data

- **Vercel** hosts the site. Every page you load is served by them, so their servers see your requests and IP address.
- **Resend** delivers email. A contact-form message passes through them on the way to my inbox.
- **Microsoft Clarity**, as above.

Each of these holds data because it needs it to do its job, and none of them get it to sell.

---

## How long I keep things

**Contact messages.** If we end up working together, your message becomes part of the project record and I keep it for the life of the project plus a year. If we don't, I delete it two years after you sent it. Ask sooner and I'll delete it sooner.

**Analytics.** Microsoft holds this on their own retention schedule, which I don't control.

**Email.** Correspondence sits in my inbox the way any business email does. I clear out old threads periodically.
