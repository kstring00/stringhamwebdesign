# What I need from you

The build moves as fast as you can get me these. Most clients find that gathering them takes an evening — and doing it before we start is the single biggest thing you can do to hit the timeline.

Send everything to me or upload it in the portal. Don't worry about formatting. Messy is fine. Missing is what slows us down.

---

## Must have before I start designing

### Your logo
The original file if you have it — usually ending in `.svg`, `.ai`, `.eps`, or `.pdf`. A big `.png` with a transparent background is fine too.

A screenshot from your Facebook page is not. If that's all you have, tell me and we'll sort it.

### Business details
- Business name exactly as you want it written
- Phone number
- Email for the contact form to go to
- Physical address, if customers come to you
- Hours
- Service area, if you go to them

### The one sentence
What you do, for whom, where. "Family dentistry in League City." "Custom cabinets for Houston-area homeowners."

If you can't say it in one sentence yet, we'll write it together on the call. But try.

### Your existing accounts
Logins for anything that's already set up:
- Domain registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)
- Google Business Profile
- Any current website or hosting
- Social media you want linked

If you don't know whether you have these, that's a normal answer. We'll figure it out.

---

## Photos

**Real photos beat stock photos every time.** A slightly imperfect picture of you and your actual shop will outperform a polished photo of a stranger.

What I'm looking for:
- **You.** One good photo of the owner or the team. Face visible, decent light. Phone camera is fine.
- **Your work.** Before-and-afters, finished projects, your space, your product. As many as you have — I'll pick.
- **Your place.** Storefront, workshop, vehicle, whatever customers will recognize.

**Send originals, not screenshots or texts.** Photos sent through text messaging get compressed and look blurry on a website. Upload them to the portal, share a Google Photos or Dropbox link, or email them at full size.

No usable photos? Tell me. We'll either plan a quick photo session or I'll direct you on taking a few that will work.

---

## Words

### Services or products
A plain list. Each one with a sentence or two on what it is and who it's for. Prices if you show them.

### About you
A few honest paragraphs. How you started, why you do this, what you care about. This doesn't need to be polished — I'll shape it. It needs to be true.

### Credentials
Licenses, certifications, years in business, insurance, associations. Spell them out; customers don't know the acronyms.

### Testimonials
Anything customers have said about you. Google reviews, texts, emails, Facebook comments. Copy them exactly — I'll ask permission before using names.

### Frequently asked questions
The five things customers always ask before hiring you. You already know what they are.

---

## If you're selling online

- Product photos, one per item minimum, on a plain background if possible
- Name, price, and a short description for each
- Shipping approach: flat rate, calculated, or local pickup
- Your Stripe account, set up in your name — I'll walk you through it

---

## Things you don't need to worry about

- **Colors and fonts.** If you have a brand, send it. If not, that's what the design step is for.
- **Writing perfectly.** Copy polish is included. Give me the raw material.
- **Knowing what pages you need.** That's what the call is for.
- **Technical anything.** Domain, hosting, email setup — I handle it, in your name, with you on the screen.

---

## The honest note

Almost every project that runs late runs late because of this list. Not the design, not the code — the photos and the words. The clients who launch on time are the ones who blocked out one evening and sent everything at once.

You can do that this week, before we even start. It'll make everything after it faster.
