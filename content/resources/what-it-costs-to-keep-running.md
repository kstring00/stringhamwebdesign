# What it costs to keep a website running

The build price is one number. Then there's the cost of keeping the site alive every month after — and a lot of people don't find out about that until the first bill shows up.

Here's the full list. Nothing on it goes to me.

---

## The two you can't skip

### Domain name — about $15/year

Your web address, rented yearly from the registrar. Turn on auto-renew and forget about it.

### Hosting — about $20/month

Where your site lives. I put every business site on Vercel's business plan, in your name, on your card.

**Why not the free plan?** Vercel's free tier is for personal, non-commercial projects only. A business site on it is against their terms, and they enforce it — sites get paused with no warning. Twenty dollars a month is the honest cost of a fast, secure, always-on business website. That's less than most people spend on coffee.

**Running total for a basic site: roughly $255 a year.**

---

## The ones that depend on what you're doing

### Business email — about $7/user/month

If you want `you@yourbusiness.com` instead of a Gmail address. Optional, but it makes you look like a real business and it's what I'd recommend for anyone who emails customers.

### Payments — 2.9% + 30¢ per sale

Only if the site sells things. Stripe takes its cut from each transaction and sends the rest to your bank. No monthly fee, no cost when you're not selling.

### Booking tools — $0 to $30/month

If you take appointments online, the scheduling tool has its own price. Some are free at low volume. We'll pick one that fits during scoping.

### A content system — $0 to $25/month

If you'll be adding blog posts or updating pages yourself regularly, a content system makes that easy. If you'll rarely touch the site, you don't need one and I won't sell you one.

---

## What's free

- **Security certificate (the padlock)** — included with hosting, renews itself.
- **Google Business Profile** — free, and often worth more than the site.
- **Search Console** — free.
- **Backups** — every version of your site is saved automatically. No add-on.

## The honest comparison

| | Site with me | Wix / Squarespace |
|---|---|---|
| Build | One-time, fixed | Free, you do it |
| Monthly | ~$20 hosting | $16–$50/month |
| Domain | ~$15/year | ~$15/year, or "free" the first year then full price |
| Who owns it | You | Them, as long as you keep paying |
| If you stop paying | Site keeps running on your hosting | Site disappears |

Over three years, the monthly costs come out close. The difference is what you have at the end of it.
